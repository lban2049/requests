# Community & Contribution

Requests is one of the most widely used Python packages, with approximately 30 million downloads per week and over 1,000,000 repositories depending on it. This robust community is a testament to the collective efforts of its contributors. This section provides information on the project's origins, its maintainers, and how you can engage with and contribute to the Requests project.

## Project Origins and Leadership

Requests was initially created and lovingly developed by Kenneth Reitz.

The project is maintained by dedicated individuals known as the "Keepers of the Crystals." The current keepers are Nate Prewitt and Seth M. Larson.

Previous significant contributors who served as keepers include:

*   Kenneth Reitz
*   Cory Benfield
*   Ian Cordasco

![Kenneth Reitz](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/psf/requests/main/ext/kr.png)

Requests is also supported by the Python Software Foundation:

![Python Software Foundation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/psf/requests/main/ext/psf.png)

## Patches and Suggestions

The development of Requests benefits greatly from the community's contributions, including numerous patches and suggestions from a wide array of individuals. The project's growth and stability are a direct result of these efforts.

You can see a comprehensive list of all contributors on the project's GitHub page:

[![Contributors](https://img.shields.io/github/contributors/psf/requests.svg)](https://github.com/psf/requests/graphs/contributors)

Engaging with the community and contributing to the project can involve reporting issues, suggesting improvements, or submitting code changes. Your involvement helps ensure Requests remains a reliable and powerful HTTP library.

## Engaging with the Community

We encourage you to engage with the Requests community. Whether you're reporting a bug, suggesting a new feature, or contributing code, your participation is invaluable to the project's continued success and evolution.
